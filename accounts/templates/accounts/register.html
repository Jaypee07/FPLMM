Don't know your FPL Team ID?
  1. Go to fantasy.premierleague.com and log in.
  2. Click "Leagues & Cups".
  3. Open any league you're a member of.
  4. Click on your own name in that league's standings.
  5. Look at the URL — the number after /entry/ is your FPL Team ID.
